# Product Specification

## Promise

Help a person or household understand what food they have, decide what to make, understand what is missing, and prepare to acquire it.

## V1 user journey

A stranger lands on the site, signs in, completes short preference onboarding, adds pantry items manually or submits kitchen images, confirms scan candidates, asks for a meal in natural language, reviews a structured recipe, adds missing ingredients to a shopping list, and returns to history or profile controls.

## Trust requirements

Allergies are hard constraints. Pantry scans are suggestions until confirmed. Commerce data must be verified or explicitly unavailable. The system must not present AI estimates as authoritative nutrition, expiration, or food-safety facts.
